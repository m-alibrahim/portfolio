# Personalization Research

How to find the specific facts that make outreach feel intentional, not spammy. Run these searches in parallel.

---

## Company-level hooks

### Funding & growth
- `[Company Name] funding 2025 OR 2026`
- `[Company Name] Series A OR Series B OR Series C announcement`
- `[Company Name] raised million`
- Check Crunchbase if findable via search snippets

### Expansion & hires
- `[Company Name] expansion OR new office OR opens`
- `[Company Name] hiring OR jobs OR careers` — also `web_fetch` their careers page directly
- `[Company Name] headcount growth LinkedIn`
- `[Company Name] new CEO OR CTO OR CFO` (recent leadership = new strategy)

### Press & awards
- `[Company Name] news` (last 6 months)
- `[Company Name] award OR ranked OR "best of"`
- `[Company Name] Fast 50 OR FT 1000 OR Inc 5000`

### Product / business signal
- `[Company Name] launches OR announces OR partnership`
- `[Company Name] customers OR case study` (informs the agency's pitch — "you serve X, we hire for X-serving companies")

## Decision-maker hooks

Use `Exa:web_search_exa` for these — Exa is better at semantic search of social content.

### LinkedIn activity
- `[Full Name] [Company] linkedin post`
- `[Full Name] linkedin article` — they may have authored content
- `[Full Name] comment OR repost` — what are they engaging with?

### Career signals
- `[Full Name] [Company] joined` — when did they start? new-hires <12 months are great targets (fresh mandate, building team)
- `[Full Name] previous OR ex-[Previous Company]`
- `[Full Name] alumni [University]` — shared alumni = a real connection lever

### Public profile / thought leadership
- `[Full Name] podcast OR interview OR panel`
- `[Full Name] speaker OR conference`
- `[Full Name] blog OR substack OR medium`

## Triangulation — find the "right now" moment

The strongest hooks combine company + DM + timing. Look for overlap:

| Signal A | Signal B | The hook |
|---|---|---|
| Company raised Series B | DM is Head of Engineering | "Just saw the Series B — guessing engineering is your biggest hiring challenge right now?" |
| Company opened new office | DM joined 3 months ago | "Congrats on the [city] expansion + your recent move to [Company]. Bet you're under pressure to staff up fast." |
| Open role posted for [X] | DM is hiring manager for [X] | "Saw you're hiring a [Role]. We placed two of these at [Similar Company] last quarter." |
| Recent layoffs at competitor | Your prospect is hiring | "[Competitor] just let go of 30 engineers — there's a window to grab senior talent. We've already spoken to a few." |
| DM posted about a hiring pain | (anything) | Lead with the post — quote their own words back, then offer the fix |

## What counts as a verified fact

For Dream 100 outreach, the bar is "publicly verifiable, recent (last 12 months), specific."

✅ GOOD: "Saw your Series B announcement in TechCrunch last month" (specific, sourced, recent)
✅ GOOD: "Read your LinkedIn post about scaling engineering hiring in November" (specific, sourced, recent)
✅ GOOD: "Noticed you're hiring 4 backend engineers based on your careers page" (specific, sourced, current)

❌ BAD: "Saw you're doing exciting things at [Company]" (generic, unsourced)
❌ BAD: "I've been following [Company] for a while" (lazy, signals nothing)
❌ BAD: "I think we have great synergies" (corporate boilerplate)

## If you can't find a specific hook

Some prospects are quiet — small companies, low-public-profile DMs, etc. Three fallbacks:

1. **Ask the user** for inside info. They may know the prospect via mutual connections, candidate conversations, or local community.
2. **Use a category hook** — instead of company-specific, anchor in the niche: "Most Series B fintech CTOs we work with are dealing with [specific pain]." Less personal, but still relevant.
3. **Lead with a value asset** — skip the personalization and offer a strong piece of value (salary guide, candidate spec-in). The value itself becomes the hook.

Surface the fallback choice to the user in the brief — don't silently default to generic.

---

## Time-box

Research one prospect: 5-10 minutes max. Beyond that, diminishing returns. Lock 3-5 facts and start writing.

If you've spent 15+ minutes and still have nothing usable, the prospect is genuinely low-signal — note this in the output and use a category hook + value asset approach.
