# Dream 100 Scoring Rubric (Recruitment)

Use this to score each candidate company 1-10. A company makes the Dream 100 list at **score 6+**.

## The 5 core dimensions

Each scored 0-2. Sum = fit score (max 10).

### 1. Repeat hiring (0-2)
- **0** — No visible open roles or hiring history in the agency's niche
- **1** — 1-2 open roles matching, or hires sporadically
- **2** — 3+ open roles matching the agency's titles, OR strong history of hiring in this niche (e.g. 20+ hires in similar roles in past 12 months visible on LinkedIn)

### 2. Growth signal (0-2)
- **0** — Flat, declining, or no public growth data
- **1** — Modest growth (single-digit headcount %, older funding)
- **2** — One or more of:
  - Headcount +20% in 12-18 months (LinkedIn Insights)
  - Series A/B/C/D in last 18 months
  - Announced office expansion, new market, M&A
  - Featured in industry "top growing" / "hot 100" list this year
  - Job postings count up significantly

### 3. Attractive employer (0-2)
- **0** — Bad Glassdoor (<3.0), known reputational issues, recent layoffs
- **1** — Average employer signals
- **2** — Glassdoor 4.0+, well-known positive brand, strong perks/equity, candidates known to want to work there

### 4. Pay & budget (0-2)
- **0** — Below market, government salary bands, or known cheap on agencies
- **1** — Market-rate, some agency usage
- **2** — Pays market or premium; uses agencies; has budget for retained/exclusive

### 5. Public presence (0-2)
- **0** — Stealth, low public footprint, no leadership visible on LinkedIn
- **1** — Some public presence, leadership findable but quiet (no recent activity)
- **2** — Active LinkedIn company page, leadership posts publicly and recently, easy to identify decision-makers in principle

This is a quick LinkedIn glance — does the company project itself publicly and is the leadership team identifiable? Actual contact-finding happens in the `dream-100-contact-finder` skill.

## Interpretation

- **9-10** — Top tier. Front-load outreach. These are the 10-20 that drive 80% of revenue.
- **7-8** — Strong Dream 100. Standard cadence.
- **6** — Watchlist. Include but lower priority.
- **<6** — Drop. Don't pad the list.

## Diversification

The training emphasizes a mix:
- Some companies that work exclusively with agencies (winnable + lucrative once in)
- Some that use multiple agencies (easier to break in, lower margin)
- A few that say "we don't use agencies" (long shot but high reward — one new HR director can flip them)

Aim for roughly 60/30/10 split across the three categories. Note this in the Notes column.

## Watch-outs to flag in Notes

- Recent layoffs or hiring freeze
- Already exclusive with a known competitor (note the competitor)
- PE-owned with cost-cutting mandate
- Headquartered abroad with small local office (decision-making elsewhere)
- Founder/CEO publicly hostile to agencies (e.g. LinkedIn posts) — still include but flag
