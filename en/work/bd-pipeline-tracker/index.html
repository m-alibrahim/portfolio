Internal system

BD pipeline tracker

A lightweight system for a business development team: accounts, opportunities by stage, tasks, and contracts with collections in one place.

Try the demo

The problem

Opportunities live in spreadsheets and chats, and follow-up depends on each person's memory. Good leads go cold because nobody called, and management can't see where things stand or what has actually been collected.

How it works

  1. The Today page shows what needs attention now: late tasks and overdue follow-ups.
  2. The board moves each opportunity between stages by drag and drop, from first contact to signed.
  3. After signing, it tracks payments: what has come in and what is late.

Try it yourself

Open the opportunities board and drag a card to another stage, then open contracts and collections to see the late payment. The demo interface is in Arabic.

All clients and amounts here are fictional. Your changes stay in your browser only.
