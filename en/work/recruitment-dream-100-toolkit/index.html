Dream 100 toolkit for recruitment agencies

A chain of AI skills that starts from an agency's website and ends with 100 target companies, their decision-makers, and outreach written for each one.

  3. 300 decision-makers

The problem

Recruitment agencies send one message to generic lists and disappear among hundreds like it. Building a precise list by hand takes weeks.

How it works

  1. A short interview defines the agency's ideal client.
  2. Research returns 100 companies with hiring signals and three decision-makers at each.
  3. Multichannel outreach is written for each prospect individually.
