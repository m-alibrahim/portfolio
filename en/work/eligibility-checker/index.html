Acquisition campaign

Legal campaign with an eligibility checker

A time-limited campaign built on a regulatory change, where the prospect checks their own eligibility in about a minute.

Try the demo

The problem

When a regulatory change opens a time-limited claim, many people call to ask the same thing: does this cover me? Most of those calls never become clients, and lawyers lose hours to screening.

How it works

  1. The visitor answers a few short questions in plain language, with no legal terms.
  2. A preliminary result appears straight away: eligible, not eligible, or needs review.
  3. Their answers reach the team on WhatsApp, so the lawyer starts from a screened case.

Try it yourself

Change your answers for the sale date and the seller type, and watch the result and next step change.
