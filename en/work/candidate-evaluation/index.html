Internal system

Candidate evaluation platform

A structured hiring tool: several interviewers score a candidate on shared criteria, then results are compared and exported as a report.

Try the demo

The problem

After interviews, the decision rests on scattered impressions. Each interviewer remembers something different, and candidates are never compared fairly.

How it works

  1. General criteria and role-specific ones, each with its own weight.
  2. Every interviewer scores independently and notes strengths and weaknesses.
  3. An analysis page compares candidates and exports a report.

Try it yourself

Open the analysis tab and pick a candidate to see three interviewers' scores, or add a new evaluation. The demo interface is in Arabic.

Candidates and scores are sample data, and your changes stay in your browser only.
