How I work

  1. Understand the market first

    Who buys this service, what they pay today, and who competes for it. Research and numbers before any campaign.

  2. Decide who to target

    A short, precise list by sector and size, not a long generic one.

  3. Shape the offer

    A service with a clear scope and a price the client understands, and a message written for them.

  4. Build what keeps it running

    Tracking, reporting and automation, so the work does not depend on one person.
