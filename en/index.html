About

I've worked in business development for more than six years, mostly in legal and consulting services in Riyadh, with private and government clients.

I studied Islamic law, so I understand what a lawyer offers before I put it in front of a client. I'm now studying for an MSc in artificial intelligence, and I build the tools my work needs myself: a client pipeline tracker, intake forms, and research-led campaigns.

What I care about is a result you can measure: revenue, a signed contract, or a winning bid.

Focus areas

  • Business development for legal and professional services

    Finding the right clients by sector and size, reaching them with planned campaigns, and turning the service into a clear offer: an annual retainer with a defined scope and a price people understand.

  • Tenders and proposals

    Reading the RFP, preparing the technical and financial proposal and pricing, and coordinating the bid team through to submission on Etimad.

  • Key account management

    Looking after major clients, growing the services they use, and keeping retainer usage in line with fees so the contract works for both sides.

  • Partnerships and alliances

    Bringing companies together for projects and tenders that no single firm could deliver alone.
